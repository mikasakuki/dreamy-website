2. Data collected when visiting the website

When you use our website for purely informational purposes, we only collect the data your browser automatically transmits (server log files):

4. Cookies and local storage

This website uses exclusively technically necessary cookies and localStorage entries:

  • jume_consent: Stores your cookie choice. No tracking, stays local.
  • jume_newsletter_email: Only if you enter an email in the form. Temporarily stored in the browser until submission; on newsletter sign-up, the email address is transmitted to Brevo (formerly Sendinblue).

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.

5. Google Fonts

If you have consented, this website loads fonts from Google LLC (USA). Your IP address is transmitted to Google in the process. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (your consent).

6. Newsletter (Brevo)

On newsletter sign-up, your email address is transmitted to Brevo SAS (formerly Sendinblue, based in France, EU). We use double opt-in. You can unsubscribe at any time via the unsubscribe link in the newsletter or by email to info@jume.app.

We have concluded a data processing agreement with Brevo SAS pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR. Brevo SAS is based in France (EU); data processing takes place within the EU.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (your consent).

7. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, your data is processed exclusively to handle your request and is deleted afterwards.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.

8. No tracking or analytics

This website uses no Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, no heatmaps and no other tracking services. No user profiles are created.

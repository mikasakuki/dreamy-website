Dream glossary

What does it mean to fall? What do teeth, water or animals say about your inner world? This glossary explains the 5 dream categories and 9 motif groups from the jume app — with detailed explanations of every motif. As orientation, not a rigid template.

Dream categories Fear & control Body & identity Relationships Death & change Freedom Nature Everyday Places & spaces Animals & beings

What type was your dream?

In the jume app you choose one category per dream after waking — it describes the overall character of the night.

🌙

Normal dream

An ordinary dream without a strong special character — no nightmare, no conscious control, no particular intensity. It simply unfolds. The most common category. Even normal dreams can carry valuable images or moods.

🌘

Nightmare

A dream that triggers fear, threat, panic or helplessness — and often wakes you up. Nightmares aren't "bad": they often point to themes that haven't yet found words in waking life. More frequent in stressful phases or with emotional overload.

✨

Lucid dream

You know while dreaming that you're dreaming — and have a certain degree of awareness or even control. Lucid dreaming can be trained and enables active dream work. Appears more often after interrupted sleep.

🔁

Recurring dream

The same pattern, scene or feeling appears regularly — sometimes over years. Recurring dreams almost always point to an unresolved theme asking for attention. When the theme is consciously addressed, such dreams often fade.

💓

Emotionally intense dream

Not a nightmare in the classic sense, but emotionally very charged — full of longing, grief, joy, loss or love. Such dreams often echo for a long time and leave a bodily feeling or mood that lasts all day.

What appeared in your dream?

In jume you choose several motifs from 9 groups per dream — and over time you spot patterns that become visible across weeks.

😨

Fear & loss of control

Especially common in phases of pressure, insecurity or overwhelm.

⬇️Falling

A sudden loss of control — the feeling of having nothing to hold on to. One of the most universally common dream motifs. Waking up while falling (a hypnic jerk) is a normal reflex while falling asleep.

🏃Being chased

Something is hunting you and you can't escape. Almost always the pursuing figure stands for something you're avoiding in waking life: a conversation, an emotion, a decision.

😨Panic

Overwhelming fear without a clear cause — the feeling of being unable to act while something threatening happens. Often paired with a racing heart and abrupt waking.

🗺️Getting lost

You're looking for a place, can't find it or suddenly don't know where you are. Disorientation in life, an unclear decision or the feeling of having lost your own path.

🔒Being trapped

You can't get out or you're being held. Can point to the feeling of having no freedom to act in a situation, relationship or phase of life.

🫚Being paralyzed

You want to move or flee, but your body won't obey. A classic form of sleep paralysis that can carry into dreams. Stands for helplessness and being unable to act.

😶Being unable to speak

You try to call out, but no sound comes. Often for the feeling of not being heard or of not being able to express yourself in a situation.

⏰Being late

You miss a train, a plane, an exam — even though you tried everything. A classic performance-anxiety motif in phases with a lot of responsibility or the worry of not being enough.

📉Failing

You fail at something — a task, an exam, an expectation. Often linked to fear of judgment or the inner belief of not being good enough.

💨Fleeing

You run away — sometimes successfully, sometimes not. Unlike "being chased", the focus here is on your own movement and the wish to escape.

🌀Loss of control

Something spins out of control — a car, a situation, the forces of nature. A general motif for phases of life where you feel at the mercy of how things unfold.

🌊Being overwhelmed by something huge

A wave, darkness, a crowd — something bigger than you rolls toward you. Stands for the feeling of not being equal to a situation or emotion.

💧Drowning

You go under — in water, in a situation, in feelings. One of the most intense fear motifs. Often for the feeling of being overwhelmed by emotions, expectations or responsibility and running out of air.

🦷

Body & identity

These motifs circle self-image, health, vulnerability and the question: who am I?

🦷Teeth falling out

Probably the best-known dream motif worldwide. Linked to fear of loss, insecurity about one's appearance and loss of control. Sometimes also the feeling of not being able to express yourself clearly. Physical teeth-grinding can trigger tooth dreams too.

💇Losing hair

Hair stands for strength, vitality and self-image. Losing it in a dream can be linked to loss of control, physical exhaustion or the fear of losing your presence.

🩹Injury or illness

You're injured or seriously ill — or someone else is. Can point to physical exhaustion, emotional pain or an inner vulnerability you haven't yet fully acknowledged in waking life.

🪞Mirror or a different appearance

You see yourself differently than in waking life — younger, older, disfigured or more beautiful. Touches themes of identity, self-image and how others perceive you.

😳Being naked

You're naked — and no one else is. A motif for vulnerability and the feeling of being exposed or judged. Sometimes also freeing: taking off every mask.

🔇Losing your voice

You try to speak or sing, but your voice fails. Closely related to "being unable to speak" — the focus here is on losing an ability that's otherwise taken for granted.

🦋Body transforming

You become something or someone else — an animal, another person, something unknown. A powerful image for an inner transformation already underway.

🤰Pregnancy

Rarely a literal premonition — more often an image for something taking shape in you: a project, a new phase, a part of you not yet fully developed.

👶Baby or child

Often a symbol for a younger self, for vulnerability, innocence or something that still needs care. For parents it can also be a direct processing of worries.

🎭Doppelgänger

You meet yourself. A particularly intense motif for inner division, suppressed parts or the question of your own identity. What does your double do that you don't do in waking life?

🧓Aging or changing

You suddenly see yourself much older or younger — linked to time pressure, transience or the wish to bring back or let go of a certain phase of life.

👻Being invisible

No one sees or hears you. A mirror for the waking feeling of being overlooked, ignored or not taken seriously.

💔

Relationships & people

People in dreams often represent parts of ourselves — or stand for themes connected to them.

💔Ex-partner

Rarely a sign that you miss them. In dreams, ex-partners often stand for a theme of that relationship: security, freedom, trust, loss. The dream is less about the person than about what they represent.

😤Argument

Unspoken conflicts or unresolved feelings toward this person — or a part of you they represent — seek expression in the dream.

🌫️A stranger

You're with someone you don't know — yet feel very familiar with in the dream. Such figures often represent parts of yourself or qualities you're developing.

👧Someone from childhood

A person from your childhood — often one who was formative. Such dreams invite you to look at childhood patterns, early experiences or relationship dynamics that are still at work.

😢Losing someone

Someone is suddenly gone — you can't find them or they disappear. Often linked to fear of loss, grief or the wish for more closeness in a relationship.

🤝Reunion

You meet someone again you haven't seen for a long time. Often linked to longing, processing or the wish for connection with this person or what they embody.

💛Closeness or love

Intense feelings of closeness, connection or being in love. Often less about a real person than about a deep need for connection — with yourself or others.

👩Mother

Often a symbol for security, care and home — but also for dependence or unprocessed childhood experiences. How do you relate to her in the dream?

👨Father

The father often stands for authority, rules, protection or strictness. Dreams with the father frequently circle performance, expectations or the search for recognition.

👫Friends

Close friends in a dream can mirror real relationships or stand symbolically for qualities you associate with them: lightness, loyalty, humor, safety.

👨‍👩‍👧Family

Family dynamics in a dream often mirror deep imprints and early relationship patterns. What role do you take in the dream's family?

💍Wedding

An image for union, commitment and transition. Weddings in dreams often stand for the inner joining of two parts within you or a far-reaching decision.

🕊️A deceased person

You meet someone who has died — often very alive and real. Frequently for processing grief, but also experienced as a deep inner encounter. Many report feeling calm and connected afterwards.

🎤A famous person

Dreams of celebrities have little to do with the person themselves. They usually stand for qualities you admire or wish for: creativity, courage, strength, visibility.

💀

Death & transformation

Death in a dream is almost never literal — it almost always stands for transition, ending and change.

💀Death

Usually not an omen but a symbol for the end of a phase or the dying of an old self. Almost always a sign of change — not of loss.

⚰️Funeral

An image for conscious letting go, closure or grief work — not necessarily about a person, but about a phase, a hope or a version of yourself.

👋Farewell

You say goodbye to someone — for good or temporarily. Often linked to grief, letting go of what was, or the end of a phase of life.

👻Ghosts

Something from the past is still present — a person, a memory, an unresolved theme. Ghosts in dreams almost always want to communicate something; they're rarely threatening once you look at them.

🌑Shadow figure

A dark, formless figure. In Jungian psychology, the "shadow" — suppressed parts of the personality seeking integration.

🌍End of the world

The world ends — apocalypse, catastrophe, the end of everything. Often an expression of the feeling that something is changing fundamentally right now. Common in phases of major upheaval.

🌱Rebirth

After darkness comes light, after an ending comes a beginning. A clear image for inner renewal, a fresh start and the emergence of something new from the old.

🩸Blood

Meaning depends on context: injury, life energy, passion or loss. A little blood is often less unsettling than a lot. Sometimes stands for emotional pain, sometimes for strength.

🚀

Freedom & control

These motifs often feel powerful, vast or magical — and are among the most beautiful dreams.

🦅Flying

The classic freedom motif. You lift off the ground — sometimes effortlessly, sometimes with effort. Stands for lightness, overcoming limits and a change of perspective.

☁️Floating

Gentler than flying — you float without actively steering. Stands for inner peace and letting go. Sometimes also dissociation: gliding lightly over things without being truly grounded.

⚡Superpowers

You can fly, move things, heal or are invincible. A wish for agency — often in phases where you feel powerless or restricted.

🔮Control over your surroundings

You can shape the dream world at will or summon things. Often a sign of lucid dreaming or of growing self-efficacy in waking life.

🌑Being invisible

You're invisible to others — this time freeing. Sometimes a wish to withdraw, sometimes observing without judgment.

⚡Teleportation

You move instantly from place to place. Can stand for the wish for quick solutions or the ability to skip over obstacles.

🧱Walking through walls

You pass through obstacles blocked to others. A powerful image of freedom — limits that apply to others don't apply to you.

🚀Being in space

You float among the stars or see the Earth from above. Linked to the search for meaning and the feeling of being small and vast at once.

🌊

Nature & elements

Water, fire, earth and air appear in dreams with surprising frequency — each element carries its own emotional quality.

💧Water

One of the most meaning-rich dream motifs. Stands for the unconscious and the flow of feelings. Clear water = clarity, murky = confusion, stormy = overwhelm.

🌊Sea or ocean

The sea stands for the unconscious in all its depth — infinite, sometimes calm, sometimes stormy. Standing on the shore often means standing at a threshold.

🌊Flood

The water rises or a wave rolls in. A classic overwhelm motif — an emotion or situation you don't feel prepared for.

🔥Fire

Can mean passion, destruction, transformation or warmth. A controllable fire stands for inner energy, an uncontrollable one for anger or loss.

🌧️Rain

Rain can feel cleansing and freeing — or heavy and sad. Warm rain often stands for emotional cleansing, cold rain for sorrow or inner emptiness.

❄️Snow

Stillness, cold, purity — or being frozen. Snow can stand for inner calm, but also for emotional distance or the freezing of feelings.

⛈️Storm

Inner tension, charged emotions or an approaching conflict. A storm that clears can feel very freeing.

🌲Forest

The forest stands for the unconscious and the uncontrollable — but also for protection and shelter. A bright forest symbolizes clarity, a dark one the unknown.

⛰️Mountains

Goals, challenges, perspective. Climbing a mountain stands for ambition or overcoming. Standing on the summit for clarity and a wide view.

🌙Moon

The moon stands for the unconscious, intuition, the feminine and cycles. A glowing full moon can mean clarity and intensity, a veiled one the hidden.

☀️Sky

An open sky stands for possibility, freedom and transcendence. A covered or threatening sky for oppression or the feeling that something is coming.

🌑Darkness

Deep darkness in which you see nothing. Can symbolize insecurity, the unknown or the unconscious. Sometimes oppressive, sometimes protective like a cloak.

✨Light

Sudden light, glowing apparitions or rays. Stands for insight, hope or a spiritual experience. In nightmares, light can be saving.

🌍Earthquake

The ground breaks open. A powerful image for the feeling that something fundamental is being shaken: security, relationships, identity or values.

🏃

Everyday & movement

Everyday situations that warp strangely in the dream — often a mirror for pressure or the feeling of not getting anywhere.

🐌Running, but slowly

You try to run fast, but your body moves as if in slow motion. One of the most common stress motifs — the feeling of trying hard but not really moving forward.

🚗Vehicle out of control

The car, bus or train moves without your doing or you can't brake. A classic loss-of-control motif for the feeling of no longer steering your own life.

🚂Missing a train or flight

You're too late, the train is just leaving. The fear of missing an opportunity or of not being where you should be.

📝School or exam

You sit an exam you're not prepared for. Fear of judgment and the feeling of not being enough. Often still appears decades after school.

💼Work or office

You're at work — usually in an unpleasant situation. Processing work stress, hierarchy questions or unfinished tasks.

📱Phone or message

You can't be reached, an important call won't connect or you can't dial the number. Often linked to the feeling of losing connection.

✈️Airport

A transitional space par excellence. Departure, waiting, farewell — or missing the flight. Stands for transitions in life and sometimes the fear of letting go.

🔄Walking in circles

You walk but arrive nowhere — the path keeps leading back to the same point. An image for a problem going round and round, or a situation with no clear way out.

🧱Making no progress

You try to do something, but it just won't move forward — obstacles keep appearing. A transfer of the feeling of stagnation onto the dream world.

🚦Traffic and gridlock

You're stuck in traffic or can't get through. Similar to "making no progress" — frustration about outer circumstances blocking your progress.

🏠

Places & spaces

Where you are in the dream is often just as meaningful as what happens.

🏠House or apartment

In a dream, the house almost always stands for the psyche itself. Different rooms = different aspects of the personality. Which room are you in? What do you find there?

🚪An unknown room

In a familiar house you discover a room you didn't know. A beautiful symbol for new inner potential and still-undiscovered parts of the personality.

⬇️Basement

The basement classically stands for the unconscious — repressed feelings, old memories. Fear of the basement often mirrors fear of what lies there.

🪜Stairs

Stairs going up stand for ascent and growth. Stairs going down for descending into the unconscious. What happens on the stairs in the dream?

📦Elevator

Getting stuck in the elevator, rising uncontrollably or a crash — elevators stand for control over rising and falling and social or professional movement.

🌀Labyrinth

Corridors without a way out that keep leading back to the same point. A strong symbol for a problem without a clear solution or a situation where the exit is hidden.

🚪Door or locked door

Doors stand for transitions and possibilities. A locked door symbolizes something unreachable. What lies behind the door? Do you want to open it?

🏨Hotel

A temporary place to stay — you're here for a time, not at home. Stands for transitional phases, the feeling of impermanence or not having arrived yet.

🌉Bridge

A connection between two states, places or phases. Crossing a bridge stands for the transition from the old to the new. A breaking bridge for the fear of that transition.

🌆An unfamiliar city

You're somewhere you don't know but experience as meaningful. New places in a dream can symbolize inner spaces of possibility — still-unknown areas of life.

🕌A sacred place

Church, temple, holy site — a space of stillness and spirituality. Stands for the search for meaning, deep inner calm or grappling with the big questions.

🏫Your childhood home

Back in the house of childhood — familiar, but often changed. Stands for early imprints, the inner child and grappling with the past.

🐍

Animals & beings

Animals represent instincts, fears, strengths or unconscious forces. The animal's behavior — and how you deal with it — is often more meaningful than the animal itself.

🐕Dog

Loyalty, faithfulness, unconditional affection. An aggressive dog can stand for a threatened protective instinct, a playful one for joy of life and easy connection.

🐱Cat

Independence, intuition, the mysterious. A threatening cat can stand for uncontrolled impulses, a playful one for lightness. In many cultures cats are seen as a link to the dream world.

🐍Snake

One of the most ambiguous animals in dreams: healing, wisdom, transformation (shedding skin) — but also betrayal, danger or seduction. How do you relate to the snake in the dream?

🕷️Spider

Creativity, weaving structures and networks — but also the feeling of being caught in a web. A spider building its web stands for constructive power.

🐴Horse

Strength, freedom, wildness and life energy. A wild horse stands for untamed inner power. Riding a horse for control over that energy. A horse escaping for something you can't hold on to.

🐺Wolf

Wildness, instinct, strength and the pack. A wolf can mean protection or threat. In many cultures a power animal for inner strength and suppressed wild parts.

🦅Bird

Freedom, spirit and transcendence. An eagle for strength and a wide view. Small birds for lightness or vulnerability. An injured bird for restricted freedom.

🐟Fish

Fish live in water — the unconscious. They stand for subconscious content and thoughts swimming deep below the surface. Catching a fish often means grasping something unconscious.

🐛Insect

A swarm of insects often stands for small annoyances that accumulate, or inner restlessness. Bees can also stand for community and creative power.

👹Monster or creature

A strange, threatening being without a clear form. Almost always it represents a suppressed inner part — anger, fear, shame. Approaching the monster instead of fleeing often leads to its dissolution.

🌑Dark figure

A formless, dark figure — in Jungian psychology the "shadow". Suppressed parts of the personality seeking integration.

🦁Lion

Strength, courage, dignity and leadership. The lion stands for inner sovereignty. An attacking lion can stand for a force you fear or suppress in waking life.

🐯Tiger

Raw power, passion, instinct. A tiger can represent the wild, untamed side of your own energy — or an external threat coming dangerously close.

🦊Fox

Cunning, adaptability and cleverness. The fox stands for navigating difficult situations intelligently — sometimes also for deception or the feeling of being fooled.

🐘Elephant

Memory, wisdom, family bonds and steadiness. An elephant in a dream can point to something you can't let go of — or to deep inner strength you don't yet fully acknowledge.

🦉Owl

Wisdom, seeing the hidden and intuition. The owl sees in the dark — a strong symbol for knowledge only accessible in the unconscious or in stillness.

🦋Butterfly

Transformation, change, lightness and transience. The butterfly undergoes a complete metamorphosis — a clear image for inner processes just completed or beginning.

🐸Frog

Transformation, cleansing and a fresh start. Frogs live between water and land — a symbol for transitional states and the ability to adapt to entirely new conditions.

🐊Crocodile or alligator

Ancient instincts, danger from the depths. The crocodile surfaces from the water — an image for the unconscious pushing abruptly to the surface. Stands for raw primal forces and hidden threats.

🦈Shark

Predatory instinct, threat from the depths, survival. Sharks in dreams often stand for a danger that stays invisible until it's very close — sometimes also for intense competition or hostile environments.

🐦‍⬛Raven or crow

Intelligence, message and the hidden. Ravens are seen in many cultures as messengers between worlds. In a dream they can point to something you haven't consciously recognized yet.

🐻Bear

Strength, protection, motherliness — but also aggression and threat. A bear attacking you can stand for a force you shy away from in waking life. A sleeping bear for resting strength.

🦌Deer or fawn

Grace, gentleness and the wild at once. The stag unites noble strength with vulnerability. Deer often stand for the inner child or for something untouched that wants protecting.

🐬Dolphin or whale

Intelligence, playfulness and deep connection. Dolphins stand for joy and gentle strength. Whales for deep emotional experience and diving into the unconscious.

🐉Dragon

Mythical power, inner fire, wisdom or threat — depending on context. In Eastern tradition a protective being with cosmic energy. In Western dreams often an inner force to be mastered or a pent-up fire.

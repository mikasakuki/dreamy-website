Privacy

Delete account

How to permanently delete your jume account and all associated data.

Delete your account in the app

You can delete your account and all stored data at any time directly in the jume app:

  1. Open the jume app
  2. Tap the gear icon (settings) at the top right
  3. Tap your profile name at the very top
  4. Scroll down to "Delete account"
  5. Confirm the deletion

All your dream entries, wellbeing data, astrology profile and account data are thereby irreversibly removed from our servers.

Delete your account by email

If you don't have access to the app, you can also request the deletion of your account by email:

info@jume.app

Please include the email address you registered with at jume. We process your request within 30 days (Art. 17 GDPR).

What is deleted?

  • Your user account and email address
  • All dream entries and recordings
  • Wellbeing and check-in data
  • Astrology profile and birth data
  • All AI-generated content of your account

Active subscriptions are not automatically cancelled by deleting your account. Please cancel your subscription beforehand directly in the App Store or Google Play.

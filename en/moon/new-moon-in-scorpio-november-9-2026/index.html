New Moon in Scorpio on November 9, 2026

On November 9, 2026 at 08:02 (CET), sun and moon stand together in Scorpio – the darkest new moon of the year and, at the same time, its deepest fresh start. It's a night that doesn't stay on the surface: it asks what is real – and what may go, so that something new can begin.

A new moon is always a beginning – the darkest point of the lunar cycle, when the sky seems empty and yet already carries everything to come. It is the moment of sowing: what you place into the darkness now begins to grow in secret. And because this new moon stands in Scorpio, it carries an unusual depth. Scorpio is the sign of transformation: of what lies beneath the surface, of what is honest, of what dies and is reborn.

Because sun and moon are united here in Scorpio, there is no opposite in this night, only a concentrated turn inward: into the depths, into the true, into the unspoken. New moons are quiet – yet this one carries a still force. It asks: what in you finally wants to be seen honestly? And what are you still keeping in the dark, even though it has long been pressing toward the light?

Understanding the energy of Scorpio

Scorpio is a water sign and is ruled by Pluto, the principle of depth and transformation – traditionally also by Mars, which gives it a quiet determination. Unlike the bubbling feelings of Cancer or the wide waves of Pisces, Scorpio's water flows deep and dark, to where things truly matter. Scorpio doesn't want to chat – Scorpio wants to know. It is the part in us that cannot stay on the surface, that seeks closeness that is real and truth that holds.

And like every sign, Scorpio has its shadow side too: control, holding on, the mistrust that would rather keep everything in its own hands. A new moon in Scorpio can touch exactly that – and therein lies its gift. For true transformation doesn't begin with controlling more, but with releasing something you no longer need to hold.

What a new moon does

If the full moon is the harvest, then the new moon is the sowing – a beginning in the dark, long before anything becomes visible. It doesn't invite release, but setting: an intention, a direction, a quiet yes. A new moon in Scorpio does this in a deep, honest way. It shows itself less on the outside than within – in what you have long sensed but not yet spoken.

Not everything that is dark is dangerous. Some of it has simply not yet come to light.

What this can mean for your dreams

Around the new moon, dreams are often quieter and harder to grasp than at the full moon – yet in Scorpio they gain intensity and depth. The dark moonlight draws the images inward, to where the soul becomes honest. Often appearing:

  • Depth and water: dark lakes, the sea, diving, undercurrents – dreams that pull you down
  • Transformation and renewal: death and rebirth, shedding skin, doors that close so others can open
  • The hidden: secrets, cellars, what lies behind a door, something concealed becoming visible
  • Closeness and intensity: encounters that go under the skin, merging, passion, vulnerability
  • Control and letting go: wanting to hold on, power, powerlessness, the struggle for trust
  • The shadow: feelings you push away by day – anger, jealousy, longing – that turn honest at night

If your dream circled around depth, transformation or something hidden – that is typical of a Scorpio new moon. Write it down. Especially what felt uncanny or too intense in the dream often shows the very truth that wants to be seen in waking life.

What this new moon says about transformation

Scorpio is the sign of transformation – of death-and-becoming. This new moon is therefore more than a fresh start: it is an invitation to let something come to an end so something new can begin. Not every beginning is loud. Sometimes the bravest new start is honestly admitting that a phase is over. What in your life wants to shed its skin? Which old skin has long grown too tight?

What this new moon says about honesty

Because sun and moon stand together in Scorpio, this night is not about softening the edges, but about truth. Where do you keep something in the dark – from others or from yourself – because the light would be uncomfortable? A Scorpio new moon gently brings such things to the surface: a feeling you didn't want to admit, a longing, a boundary. Not to expose you, but to free you. Sometimes the most honest sentence is also the most relieving: "This is how it really is."

What this time is good for

A new moon in Scorpio is well suited for:

  • setting an honest intention – not what sounds good, but what is truly real
  • looking at what you've left in the dark too long – a feeling, a truth, a theme
  • consciously ending something that is long over – to make room for the new
  • a moment alone with yourself: a candle, silence, an honest word in a notebook
  • writing down what wants to shed its skin in you – and what may come after

Gentle company for this night

Scorpio is water – and deep water needs protection, stillness and the feeling of being safe to go within. What can fit well:

  • consciously coming to rest before sleep – lighting a candle, dimming the light, breathing inward
  • something grounding: a warm shower, a blanket, a protected space without distraction
  • breathing slowly and deeply when feelings rise – water may flow, it needn't be held
  • capturing the dreams of the following nights – here the dark, intense images carry especially much

Questions that can accompany you through this night

  • What in me finally wants to be seen honestly?
  • What am I keeping in the dark, though it wants to come to light?
  • Which old skin has grown too tight for me?
  • What may come to an end so something new can begin?

Conclusion

The New Moon in Scorpio on November 9, 2026 is a deep, honest invitation to seek a fresh start not on the surface, but where it truly matters. It is not a light, airy start, but a setting from the depths: an intention that is real, a truth that holds, a letting go that frees. Dreams of this night are often intense and hidden – yet they carry a question worth hearing: what in you wants to transform so that you can become whole again?

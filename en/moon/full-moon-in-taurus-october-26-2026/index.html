Full Moon in Taurus on October 26, 2026

On October 26, 2026 at 05:11 (CET), the full moon stands in Taurus, while the sun stands opposite in Scorpio. It's an earthy, sensual night – in which the question of holding on and letting go comes to a head.

A full moon is always a peak – a coming-to-fullness, a moment of maximum brightness. What has quietly grown over the last weeks now comes to light. And that this full moon stands in Taurus gives it an unusually calm, grounded strength. Taurus is the sign of steadiness: the body, the senses, what remains when the storm settles.

Because the moon stands in Taurus and the sun opposite in Scorpio, an axis opens up this night: between security and transformation, between the wish to hold and the need to let go. Full moons make such tensions visible. This one asks: what are you holding on to because it gives you security – and how much of it may you now let go?

Understanding the energy of Taurus

Taurus is an earth sign and is ruled by Venus – but unlike airy Libra, the Venus in Taurus lives through the senses: touch, taste, warmth, calm. Taurus does not want to be rushed. Taurus wants to feel that the ground holds. It is the part in us that seeks steadiness, that savours, that knows good things take time. No other sign can relax so deeply.

And like every sign, Taurus has its shadow side too: stubbornness, clinging, the fear of change. A full moon in Taurus can stir up exactly this question – and that's where its chance lies. Because sometimes we mistake holding on for security, when true security comes from within, not from what we clutch.

What a full moon does

If the new moon is the sowing, then the full moon is the harvest – and sometimes the breaking point. It ripens, makes visible, releases. Feelings well kept during the day push to the surface. A full moon in Taurus does this in a quiet, bodily way: it shows itself less in the head than in the belly, in the tension of the shoulders, in what the body has long known.

Security is not what you hold on to. Security is what remains when you open your hands.

What this can mean for your dreams

Around the full moon, dreams are often vivid, intense and emotionally charged – the full moonlight draws the images outward. Taurus colours them earthy, sensual and physical. Common themes are:

  • Body and senses: food, touch, warmth, taste – dreams you can almost feel
  • Home and ground: your own bed, a garden, earth, a place where you are safe
  • Possession and worth: money, things, losing or finding something, the question "is this mine?"
  • Calm and slowness: images that stand still, warm and heavy, without haste
  • Holding on and letting go: clutching something that slips away – or releasing something with relief
  • Nature and animals: meadows, cattle, trees, all that is grown and steady

If your dream circled around security, the body or holding on to something – that's typical of a Taurus full moon. Write it down. What you didn't want to let go of in the dream often shows where a change is due in waking life.

What this full moon says about security

The Taurus–Scorpio axis is the axis of having and letting go, of security and depth. In Scorpio season, things go beneath the surface: to what truly moves us, to closeness, control, transformation. This full moon brings the counterweight: it grounds. It reminds you that you have solid ground, precisely when much is in motion inside. And it gently asks whether the security you cling to still carries you – or already holds you back.

What this full moon says about letting go

Because the sun stands in Scorpio, it's not only about calm, but also about depth. Where do you hold on to something that is long over – out of fear of the emptiness afterwards? A Taurus full moon can bring old attachments to the surface: to things, to habits, to people, to an image of security. Not to take something from you, but to make room for something more real. Sometimes the most valuable sentence is: "This may go now."

What this time is good for

A full moon in Taurus is well suited to:

  • coming to rest and consciously savouring – food, warmth, touch, stillness
  • sensing what truly gives you security – and what is only habit
  • letting go of something you have held too long – an object, a worry, an expectation
  • involving the body: a warm bath, slow stretching, standing barefoot on the ground
  • writing down what you hold on to – and what would happen if you opened your hands

Gentle company for this night

Taurus is earth – and earth wants calm, warmth and slow breathing. What can fit well:

  • before sleep, feeling the body – feet, breath, the weight in the bed
  • something warm and nourishing: a tea, a blanket, a quiet moment without a screen
  • breathing slowly and deeply into the belly when feelings rise – earth holds, it needn't cling
  • holding on to the dreams of the following nights – the still, bodily images carry a lot

Questions to accompany you through this night

  • What am I holding on to because it promises me security?
  • Which of it still carries me – and which only holds me?
  • Where do I mistake habit for safety?
  • What would grow lighter if I opened my hands?

Conclusion

The Full Moon in Taurus on October 26, 2026 is an earthy, sensual invitation to come to rest – and to look honestly at what truly gives you security. It's not a call to let go at any price, but to discern: between what carries you and what you only cling to. Dreams of this night are often still and physical, yet they carry a question worth hearing: where may you open your hands and trust that the ground will hold you?

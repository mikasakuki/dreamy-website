New Moon in Leo on August 12, 2026

On August 12, 2026 at 19:36 (CEST), sun and moon stand together in Leo – united in the fire of the heart. A new moon is always a beginning. This one asks you something bold: what in you finally wants to be seen?

A new moon is the darkest night of the month. No moonlight, no looking outward – the sky withdraws, and often we do too. It's the time of sowing: what begins quietly now grows into the light over the coming weeks. And that this fresh start happens of all places in Leo gives it a very special note.

Because Leo is the sign of the heart. Of self-expression, joy, creativity and the courage to be fully yourself. Where most new moons pull inward, this one whispers something different: dare to become visible.

Understanding the energy of Leo

Leo is a fire sign ruled by the sun – the center around which everything revolves. No other sign has such a natural relationship with shining. Leo doesn't want to show off, Leo wants to express. It's the child in us that dances without wondering who's watching. The creativity that arises because it wants to. The warmth that makes other people blossom.

And like every sign, Leo has its quiet side: the fear of not being enough. Of not being seen. Of having made yourself small so as not to be too much for anyone. A new moon in Leo gently brings exactly these themes to the surface.

What a new moon does

New moons are nights of intention. Not of harvest, but of sowing. What you set in motion during this time – a plan, a decision, a first step – carries the energy of the sign the moon is in. A new moon in Leo is therefore made for beginning something that makes your heart glow.

Becoming visible isn't an act of vanity. It's the courage to stop apologizing for your light.

What this can mean for your dreams

Around the new moon, dreams are often quieter and more fleeting – the dark moon pulls the images inward. All the more valuable to hold on to them before they fade. Leo colors these dreams in its own way. Often appearing:

  • Stages and the spotlight: performing, being center stage – sometimes proud, sometimes at everyone's mercy
  • Being seen or shamed: moments when someone admires you – or you feel exposed
  • Children and being a child: playing, lightness, your own younger self
  • Powerful animals: lions, cats, everything wild and proud
  • The creative: painting, singing, dancing, creating – expression that wants out of you
  • Gold, sun, warmth: glowing colors and a sense of abundance

If your dream circled around recognition, pride or vulnerability – that's typical of Leo. Write it down. Especially the feeling it left behind often says more than the action itself.

What this new moon says about visibility

Many people carry a quiet contradiction within: they long to be seen – and fear it at the same time. This new moon can illuminate exactly that spot. Where do you hold back? Which wish, which idea, which part of you do you show to no one, for fear it might be too much?

Leo reminds you: your light takes nothing away from anyone. On the contrary – it gives others permission to shine too.

What this new moon says about self-worth

Self-worth and Leo belong together. Not the loud self-worth that needs outside validation, but the quiet one: knowing that you're enough, even when no one applauds. This new moon is a good time to give yourself something you may have long expected from others – recognition, kindness, a little pride in who you are.

What this time is good for

A new moon in Leo is well suited for:

  • setting an intention to do with courage and visibility
  • beginning something creative – without a goal, just for joy
  • a first step toward a plan your heart has known for a long time
  • writing down what you're truly proud of (even if it feels strange)
  • giving yourself the recognition you've been waiting for from outside

Gentle company for this night

Leo loves warmth and a little ceremony. What can fit well:

  • lighting a candle and writing a single sentence: what do I want to let grow within me?
  • doing something that makes you shine for no reason – music, movement, color
  • placing a hand on your heart and imagining that you're listening to yourself
  • holding on to the dreams of the following nights – even the small, quiet images

Questions that can accompany you through this night

  • What in me wants to be seen – and what am I afraid of about that?
  • Where do I make myself smaller than I am, so as not to be too much for anyone?
  • What would I begin if I were sure that I'm enough?
  • What may I be a little proud of myself for today?

In closing

The New Moon in Leo on August 12, 2026 is a quiet, warm invitation to stop hiding your light. It's not a call to a big show – but to an honest beginning. Dreams of this time are often tender and fleeting, yet they carry a question worth hearing: who would you be if you were enough for yourself?

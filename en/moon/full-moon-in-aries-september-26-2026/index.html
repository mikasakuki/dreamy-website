Full Moon in Aries on September 26, 2026

On September 26, 2026 at 18:49 (CEST), the full moon stands in Aries, while the sun shines in Libra. It's a fiery, awakening night – in which the question of the self and the we comes to a head.

A full moon is always a peak – a coming-to-fullness, a moment of maximum brightness. What has grown quietly over the last few weeks now comes to light. And that this full moon stands in Aries gives it an unusually clear, courageous force. Aries is the first sign of the zodiac: the spark, the beginning, the raw "I am".

Because the moon is in Aries and the sun opposite it in Libra, an axis stretches out this night: between self-assertion and harmony, between what I need and what the relationship needs. Full moons make such tensions visible. This one asks: where have you held yourself back too long to keep the peace?

Understanding the energy of Aries

Aries is a fire sign ruled by Mars – the principle of courage, drive and assertion. No other sign acts so directly. Aries doesn't want to deliberate, Aries wants to go. It's the part of us that stands up when something is wrong. That stands up for itself. That dares the first step, even when no one leads the way.

And like every sign, Aries has its shadow side: impatience, impulsiveness, anger that comes too fast. A full moon in Aries can stir up this force – and that's exactly where its opportunity lies. Because often, hidden under the anger, is an honest boundary that should have been drawn long ago.

What a full moon does

If the new moon is the sowing, then the full moon is the harvest – and sometimes the break. It ripens, reveals, releases. Feelings kept safely stowed during the day push to the surface. A full moon in Aries is therefore a time when honesty comes more easily than usual: toward others – and above all toward yourself.

Standing up for yourself isn't selfishness. It's the honesty without which even closeness can't hold.

What this can mean for your dreams

Around the full moon, dreams are often vivid, intense and emotionally charged – the full moonlight pulls the images outward. Aries colors them fiery and direct. Often appearing:

  • Conflict and confrontation: argument, competition, finally speaking your mind
  • Running and pursuit: running, being chased or chasing, haste, urgency
  • Fire and heat: flames, warmth, something blazing or being kindled
  • Courage and setting off: a first step, a door with something new waiting behind it
  • Anger and boundaries: rage breaking through – often a sign of "this far and no further"
  • Head, face, leader motifs: Aries rules the head – images of leading, asserting yourself, being first

If your dream circled around conflict, courage or pent-up anger – that's typical of an Aries full moon. Write it down. Especially anger in a dream is rarely "bad": it often shows you where a boundary was crossed that you barely admit to yourself while awake.

What this full moon says about the self

The Aries–Libra axis is the axis of I and you. In Libra season many tend to adapt, to please everyone, to let their own wish disappear behind the peace. This full moon brings the counterweight: it reminds you that your need counts too. That harmony built on self-denial isn't real harmony.

What this full moon says about relationship

Because the sun is in Libra, it's not just about you alone but about togetherness. Where do you give too much? Where do you swallow something to avoid upsetting anyone? An Aries full moon can bring old imbalances to the surface – not to sow conflict, but to make honesty possible. Sometimes the most courageous sentence in a relationship is: "This is what I need."

What this time is good for

A full moon in Aries is well suited for:

  • saying something you've kept to yourself too long
  • setting a boundary – clear, but not hurtful
  • finding the courage for a first step you keep putting off
  • moving excess energy: exercise, a walk, something physical
  • writing down what really makes you angry – and which boundary lies beneath it

Gentle company for this night

Aries is fire – and fire wants movement, but also care. What can fit well:

  • doing something physical before sleep to ground the energy
  • writing a letter you don't have to send – to someone or to yourself
  • breathing deeply and slowly when feelings boil up – fire needs air, not an explosion
  • holding on to the dreams of the following nights – especially the intense, uncomfortable images

Questions that can accompany you through this night

  • Where do I hold back to keep the peace – and what does it cost me?
  • Which boundary should I have drawn long ago?
  • What would I do if I dared to be honest?
  • What is burning in me right now that wants to be seen?

In closing

The Full Moon in Aries on September 26, 2026 is a fiery, honest invitation to stand up for yourself – without losing your connection to others. It's not a call to conflict, but to clarity. Dreams of this night are often direct and intense, yet they carry a question worth hearing: where are you still waiting to give yourself permission to be you?

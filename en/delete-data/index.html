Privacy

Delete data

How to delete individual data without closing your entire account.

Delete individual dream entries

Every dream entry can be deleted individually:

  1. Open the jume app and go to the dream journal
  2. Tap a dream entry to open it
  3. Tap the trash icon at the top right
  4. Confirm the deletion

The entry is deleted immediately locally and — if cloud sync is active — from the server as well.

Delete birth data

You can remove your astrology profile (date, time and place of birth) in two places:

Option 1 — directly in the birth chart:

  1. Open the Moon section in the app
  2. Tap "Birth chart"
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete birth data"

Option 2 — via settings:

  1. Tap the gear icon (settings) at the top right
  2. Tap "Birth chart & astro"
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete birth data"

Delete cloud data

This option is only visible when cloud sync is enabled in the app:

  1. Open the jume app
  2. Tap the gear icon (settings) at the top right
  3. Tap "Privacy"
  4. Scroll down to "Delete cloud data"
  5. Confirm the deletion

Delete your entire account

If you want to permanently delete your account and all data, you'll find all the information on our delete-account page.

Request by email

You can also request the deletion of specific data by email (Art. 17 GDPR):

info@jume.app

Please state which data you want deleted, along with the email address of your jume account. We process your request within 30 days.
